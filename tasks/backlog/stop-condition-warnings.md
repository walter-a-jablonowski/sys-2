# Stop-condition warnings

From the activity model (§3 stop conditions, UI sample "Stop conditions").

## What

- Warnings in the detail header and in the stale review:
  - spent > estimate or cost > budget (resources exhausted)
  - remaining cost > value (worth abandoning?)
  - goal reached but not closed (progress 100 %, status not done)
- Each warning offers the ways out: redefine the goal, re-estimate, mark failed or abandoned
- Needs `activity-resources.md`, `activity-goal.md`, `activity-status.md`
